CAPY

Embedded handheld prototype and hardware-integration project.

ProjectsProject PageTechnical Notes

What I built

CAPY progressed from an Arduino Mega proof of concept with a TFT and joystick to a smaller ESP32-based architecture. As controls and peripherals grew, I replaced the joystick path with a six-button layout and added an MCP23017 I/O expander.

Tech

ESP32, C/C++, TFT display, I²C, MCP23017, physical buttons, speaker output, portable power.

Hardest problem

Moving to a smaller microcontroller solved a physical-size problem but increased GPIO pressure and made breadboard wiring harder to manage. The I/O expander and control redesign came from that constraint.

Next revision

Reduce breadboard/jumper-wire overhead, move toward a compact reproducible assembly, and use the platform to deepen work in digital logic and eventually FPGA/RTL.
