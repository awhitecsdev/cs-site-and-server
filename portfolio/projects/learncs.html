What I built

A browser-based study guide with topic navigation, search, expandable explanations, practice material, and focused lessons that connect concepts to implementation.

Tech

HTML, CSS, JavaScript.

Hardest problem

The weak version of a study site is a large list of shallow definitions. I am deliberately moving LearnCS toward fewer, stronger topics. Current deep dives focus on C++, data structures, and computer architecture.

Deep dives

  • C++: memory, lifetime, ownership, containers
  • Data Structures: choose from the workload backward
  • Computer Architecture: follow an instruction through the machine
