About Me

I’m a university student studying computer science and electrical engineering. I like working on projects that combine hardware and software, like ESP32-based devices, server setups, and web tools that help people learn.

A lot of my projects come from wanting to understand how systems work from top to bottom: from the code, to the operating system, to the network, and down to the hardware. This portfolio is one piece of that learning process.
