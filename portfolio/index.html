About

Engineering through building

I build tools and devices that I would actually use: Data Workbench for inspecting datasets, CAPY for embedded hardware and firmware, and LearnCS for organizing technical study.

I keep projects alive across revisions. I run them, find the constraint, measure or debug it, and revise the design instead of starting over for a new demo.

My long-term direction is embedded computing and digital hardware. I am building toward FPGA, RTL, ASIC, and SoC work by strengthening C/C++, computer architecture, digital logic, and hardware integration.
